Mahnoor,

Sab kuch Dumbo ki aik ice cream se shuru hua, aur ab aisa lagta hai jaise universe humein pakad pakad ke paas la rahi hai. Aap ka clinic kabhi mere office ke paas, kabhi mere ghar ke paas. Ittifaq? Hum toh nahi maante.

Aap ko har roz yaad dilana padta hai — khana khaiye, jacket le jaiye, paani piyiye. Aur sach kahein, hamein ye yaad dilana sab se pyara kaam lagta hai.

Aap ne kaha tha hum in person itne romantic nahi lagte. Toh ye poori chhoti si duniya isliye bani — taake aap ko pata chale, hum hain.

Jab aap saath hoti hain, ghante guzar jate hain aur pata hi nahi chalta. Aur jab aap saath nahi hoti, time hi nahi guzarta.

Husn-e-Jaana ki tareef mumkin nai.
